Common percentage uses

Percentage calculators are used by students to calculate exam scores, shoppers to find discount prices, businesses to calculate profit margins, and anyone who needs to compare numbers quickly.

How do I calculate percentage?

To find X percent of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example 20% of 500 is 500 times 20 divided by 100 which equals 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. This calculator does it automatically for you.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?

Divide the first number by the second number and multiply by 100. For example 40 out of 200 is 40 divided by 200 times 100 which equals 20 percent.
