How do I calculate percentage?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example 20% of 500 = 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract old value from new, divide by old value, multiply by 100. This calculator does it automatically.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?

Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example 40 out of 200 = 20%.
