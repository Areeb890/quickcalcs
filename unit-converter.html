Why use an online unit converter?

Unit converters are useful for cooking, fitness, travel, science, and everyday life. Whether you want to know your weight in pounds, your height in inches, or the temperature in Fahrenheit, this tool gives you an instant accurate answer.

How many lbs is 1 kg?

1 kilogram is equal to 2.2046 pounds. So a person weighing 70 kg weighs about 154.3 lbs.

How many cm is 1 inch?

1 inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters. So 6 feet (72 inches) is about 182.9 cm.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and then add 32. For example, 25°C × 9/5 + 32 = 77°F.
