How many lbs is 1 kg?

1 kilogram equals 2.2046 pounds. So 70 kg = about 154.3 lbs.

How many cm is 1 inch?

1 inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters. So 6 feet (72 inches) = about 182.9 cm.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 then add 32. For example 25°C × 9/5 + 32 = 77°F.
