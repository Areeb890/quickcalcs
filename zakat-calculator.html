📖 What is Zakat?

Zakat is the third pillar of Islam — an obligatory annual payment of 2.5% of total zakatable wealth above the nisab threshold.

📐 What is Nisab?

The minimum wealth a Muslim must have before Zakat becomes obligatory, based on 87.48g gold or 612g silver.

📅 When to pay?

Once a year when you have held wealth above the nisab for one full Islamic lunar year (Hawl).

How to calculate Zakat in Pakistan 2025

Add all zakatable assets — cash, savings, gold, silver, business inventory, investments. Subtract short-term debts. If the remaining amount exceeds the nisab and you've held it for one lunar year, you owe 2.5% as Zakat.

What assets are included in Zakat?

Cash, bank savings, gold and silver held as investment, business stock, shares at market value, and money owed to you. Your home, car, and personal use items are NOT included.

What is the Nisab for Zakat in Pakistan 2025?

Based on silver: approximately PKR 95,000. Based on gold: approximately PKR 1,400,000. Most scholars recommend the silver nisab.

What percentage is Zakat?

2.5% of your total net zakatable wealth above the nisab threshold.

Can I pay Zakat in installments?

Yes. You can pay monthly or in installments as long as the full amount is paid by end of your Zakat year.

Who is eligible to receive Zakat?

The poor and needy, those in debt, travelers in need, and those working to collect and distribute Zakat.

Is my house included in Zakat?

No. Your primary home and personal use items are not included in the Zakat calculation.
