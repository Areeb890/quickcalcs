📖 What is Zakat?

Zakat is the third pillar of Islam. It is an obligatory annual payment of 2.5% of your total zakatable wealth above the nisab threshold.

📐 What is Nisab?

Nisab is the minimum amount of wealth a Muslim must have before Zakat becomes obligatory. It is based on the value of 87.48g of gold or 612g of silver.

📅 When to pay?

Zakat must be paid once a year when you have held wealth above the nisab for one full Islamic lunar year (Hawl).

How to calculate Zakat in Pakistan 2025

To calculate your Zakat, add up all your zakatable assets including cash, savings, gold, silver, business inventory, and investments. Then subtract any short term debts. If the remaining amount is above the nisab threshold and you have held it for one lunar year, you owe 2.5% of that total as Zakat.

What assets are included in Zakat?

Zakat applies to cash and bank savings, gold and silver jewelry held as investment, business stock and trade goods, shares and investments at market value, and money owed to you that you expect to receive. It does not apply to your home, car, or personal use items.

What is the Nisab for Zakat in Pakistan 2025?

The nisab for 2025 based on silver is approximately PKR 95,000 and based on gold is approximately PKR 1,400,000. Most scholars recommend using the silver nisab as it includes more people and benefits the poor more. These amounts change as gold and silver prices change.

Is Zakat on gold compulsory in Pakistan?

Yes. Zakat is due on gold and silver that meets the nisab threshold and has been held for one lunar year. This includes gold jewelry unless it is used regularly for personal adornment according to some scholars.

What percentage is Zakat?

Zakat is 2.5% of your total net zakatable wealth above the nisab threshold.

Can I pay Zakat in installments?

Yes. You can pay Zakat monthly or in installments throughout the year as long as the full amount is paid by the end of your Zakat year.

Who is eligible to receive Zakat?

Zakat can be given to the poor and needy, those in debt, travelers in need, and those working to collect and distribute Zakat.

Is house included in Zakat?

No. Your primary home and personal use items like your car and clothing are not included in Zakat calculation.
