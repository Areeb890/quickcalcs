What is BMI and how is it calculated?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a simple measurement that uses your height and weight to estimate whether your weight is healthy. BMI is calculated by dividing your weight in kilograms by your height in meters squared.

Is BMI accurate?

BMI is a useful screening tool but it does not directly measure body fat. Athletes may have a high BMI due to muscle mass. Always consult a doctor for a complete health assessment.

What is a healthy BMI?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy for most adults. Below 18.5 is underweight and above 25 is overweight. Above 30 is classified as obese.

How do I calculate BMI?

Divide your weight in kg by your height in meters squared. For example a person 175cm tall weighing 70kg has a BMI of 70 divided by 1.75 squared which equals 22.9.

What BMI is overweight?

A BMI of 25 or above is considered overweight. A BMI of 30 or above is considered obese.

Can I use this for my child?

This BMI calculator is designed for adults aged 18 and over. BMI for children uses different scales based on age and gender.
