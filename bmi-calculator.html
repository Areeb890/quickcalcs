What is BMI and how is it calculated?

BMI (Body Mass Index) uses your height and weight to estimate healthy weight. It's calculated by dividing your weight in kg by your height in meters squared.

Is BMI accurate?

BMI is a useful screening tool but doesn't directly measure body fat. Athletes may have high BMI due to muscle mass. Always consult a doctor for a full health assessment.

What is a healthy BMI?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy for most adults.

How do I calculate BMI?

Divide weight in kg by height in meters squared. This calculator does it instantly.

What BMI is overweight?

A BMI of 25 or above is overweight. 30 or above is obese.

Can I use this for my child?

This is designed for adults 18+. Children use different age/gender-based scales.
