How to use this age calculator

Enter your date of birth in the first field. The second field defaults to today's date, but you can change it to calculate your age as of any specific date — useful for forms that ask "age as of [date]," like job or visa applications. Click Calculate My Age and your results appear instantly, broken down by years, months, and days, along with several other useful figures.

What exactly does this calculator show?

Beyond your basic age in years, months, and days, this tool calculates your total months lived, total weeks lived, total days lived, and total hours lived since birth. It also tells you which day of the week you were born on, and counts down the days until your next birthday. These extra details are handy for trivia, personal curiosity, or filling in less common form fields that ask for age in different units.

Why would I need my exact age calculated?

Precise age calculations come up more often than people expect. Common uses include legal documents and court forms, visa and immigration applications, insurance policy applications, school and university admissions where cutoff dates matter, medical and clinical trial eligibility, and retirement or pension eligibility checks. Doing this by hand is easy to get wrong, especially across leap years — this tool removes that risk.

How is age calculated in years, months, and days?

The calculator takes the difference between your date of birth and the reference date, first counting full years, then full months within the remaining period, then remaining days. It automatically accounts for varying month lengths and leap years, so the result is always exact — not an estimate.

Is this calculator accurate for leap year birthdays?

Yes. If you were born on February 29th, the calculator correctly handles years where February 29th doesn't exist by using the standard convention (typically March 1st in non-leap years) for birthday countdowns.

How do I calculate my age from my date of birth?

Enter your date of birth above and click Calculate My Age. The tool instantly shows your exact age in years, months, and days, plus additional details like total days lived.

How many days old am I?

After calculating, check the "Total days lived" row in the results table — it shows the exact number of days since your birth date.

Can I calculate someone else's age, like my child's or a family member's?

Yes. Just enter their date of birth in the first field. You can also adjust the second field to calculate their age as of any past or future date.

When is my next birthday?

After calculating, the highlighted box below your results shows exactly how many days remain until your next birthday and the date it falls on.

What's the difference between chronological age and this calculator's result?

They're the same thing — chronological age simply means your actual age based on your birth date, which is exactly what this calculator computes, down to the day.

Can I use this to calculate age for a legal or official document?

Yes, the calculation method used here (exact years, months, and days between two dates) matches standard conventions used on most official forms. For legally binding documents, it's still worth double-checking any date rules specific to your country or institution.

Does this calculator work on mobile?

Yes, it's fully responsive and works the same on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

Is my date of birth stored or shared anywhere?

No. All calculations happen directly in your browser — no data is sent to a server or stored anywhere.
