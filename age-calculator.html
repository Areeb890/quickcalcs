How to use this age calculator

Simply enter your date of birth in the first field. The second field is automatically set to today's date, but you can change it to any date — past or future — to calculate age at a specific point in time. Click "Calculate My Age" and your exact age appears instantly.

What does this calculator show?

This tool shows your exact age broken into years, months, and remaining days. It also displays your age in total months, weeks, days, and hours, the day of the week you were born on, and a countdown to your next birthday.

Why calculate your exact age?

Knowing your exact age is useful for filling out legal forms, visa applications, insurance documents, medical records, and school or university admissions. It's also just fun to know exactly how many days or hours you have been alive!

Is this calculator free?

Yes, this age calculator is completely free to use with no sign-up required. You can use it as many times as you like.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my age from my date of birth?

Enter your date of birth in the field above and click "Calculate My Age." The tool instantly shows your exact age in years, months, and days.

How many days old am I?

This calculator shows you exactly how many total days you have been alive under the "Total days lived" row in the results.

Can I calculate someone else's age?

Yes. Just enter their date of birth in the first field. You can also change the "calculate as of" date to find their age on any specific date.

When is my next birthday?

After calculating, the tool shows a countdown at the bottom telling you exactly how many days until your next birthday.
