How to use this GPA calculator

Enter each of your courses, select the grade you received, and enter the number of credit hours. Click "Calculate GPA" and your Grade Point Average appears instantly. You can add as many courses as you need.

What is a good GPA?

A GPA of 3.5 or above is generally considered excellent. A GPA between 3.0 and 3.5 is good. Between 2.0 and 3.0 is average. Below 2.0 may put you on academic probation at many universities.

How is GPA calculated?

GPA is calculated by multiplying each course grade points by the credit hours, adding them all together, then dividing by the total credit hours. This calculator does all of that automatically.

Can I calculate my semester GPA?

Yes. Just enter only the courses from that semester and click Calculate GPA.

What is a 4.0 GPA?

A 4.0 GPA is the highest possible GPA, meaning you received an A or A+ in all your courses.

Does this work for college and high school?

Yes. This GPA calculator works for both high school and college students using the standard 4.0 scale.
