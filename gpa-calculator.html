How to use this GPA calculator

Enter each course, select your grade, and enter credit hours. Click Calculate GPA and your result appears instantly. Add as many courses as you need.

What is a good GPA?

3.5+ is excellent. 3.0–3.5 is good. 2.0–3.0 is average. Below 2.0 may risk academic probation at many universities.

How is GPA calculated?

Each course's grade points × credit hours, summed, then divided by total credit hours. This calculator does it all automatically.

Can I calculate my semester GPA?

Yes — enter only the courses from that semester and click Calculate GPA.

What is a 4.0 GPA?

The highest possible GPA — you received an A or A+ in every course.

Does this work for college and high school?

Yes, it works for both using the standard 4.0 scale.
